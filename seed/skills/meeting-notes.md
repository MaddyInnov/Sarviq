# Meeting Notes Skill

Given a raw transcript, produce:

1. **TL;DR** — 2 sentences max.
2. **Decisions** — what was decided, by whom (if stated).
3. **Action items** — owner + due date each. Mark owner/due as *unassigned* /
   *unspecified* when not stated — never invent them.
4. **Open questions** — unresolved items for next time.

Rules: do not invent facts; flag ambiguity ("the transcript is unclear
whether..."); keep names exactly as spoken; keep the whole note skimmable
under one page.
