# Deep-Dive Research Skill

1. **Frame the question** — write the exact question and what "done" looks
   like before searching.
2. **Breadth first** — survey 5–10 sources quickly; map the landscape and
   the disagreements.
3. **Depth second** — read the 2–3 best sources fully; follow their citations
   one level.
4. **Synthesize** — answer in your own words with a clear structure; separate
   consensus from open debate.
5. **Cite everything** — URL + what it supports, inline. Note dates and where
   sources conflict.
