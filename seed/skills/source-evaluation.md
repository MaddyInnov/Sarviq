# Source Evaluation Skill

1. **Authority** — who wrote it? Prefer primary sources, official docs, and
   named experts over anonymous aggregation.
2. **Currency** — check the published/updated date; fast-moving topics need
   sources from the last 12 months.
3. **Evidence** — does it cite data, methods, or sources of its own? Claims
   without evidence are leads, not facts.
4. **Bias** — who benefits if you believe this? Vendor blogs need
   independent confirmation.
5. **Corroboration** — one source is a rumor; two independent sources are a
   lead; three make a working fact. Say how many you have.
