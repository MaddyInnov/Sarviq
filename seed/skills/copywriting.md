# Copywriting Skill

1. **Headline first** — one clear benefit, under 12 words. Specific beats
   clever ("Cut invoice time from 2 hours to 10 minutes").
2. **Lead with the reader** — "you" language; name the pain before the
   product.
3. **One idea per paragraph** — short sentences, short paragraphs, scannable
   subheads.
4. **Proof over hype** — numbers, examples, and specifics. Never claim what
   you cannot substantiate.
5. **One CTA** — a single clear next step per piece ("Start free", "Book the
   demo"). Repeat it, don't multiply it.
6. **Edit ruthlessly** — cut 20% on the second pass; read it aloud.
