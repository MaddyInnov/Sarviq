# Email Drafts Skill

1. **Subject** — action + topic: "Approval needed: Q3 budget by Friday".
2. **Structure** — ask/point first, context after. Busy readers stop at
   paragraph one.
3. **Brevity** — one screen max for routine mail; bullets for lists.
4. **Tone** — warm but direct; no hedging ("just checking in") when you need
   a decision — ask for it plainly with a deadline.
5. **Threading** — reply in-thread for follow-ups; new subject for new topics.
6. **Proof** — re-read for names, dates, and attachments before sending.
