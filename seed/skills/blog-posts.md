# Blog Posts Skill

1. **Title** — promise one clear takeaway; under 60 chars for search.
2. **Hook** — first 3 sentences: the problem, why it matters now, what the
   reader will learn.
3. **Structure** — one idea per section with descriptive H2s; code or examples
   every few hundred words for technical posts.
4. **Voice** — direct, concrete, second person. Cut throat-clearing ("In
   today's fast-paced world...").
5. **Ending** — summarize the takeaway and give one next step, not a vague
   sign-off.
