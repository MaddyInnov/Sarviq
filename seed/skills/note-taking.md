# Note Taking Skill

1. **Capture fast** — one inbox (a file, an app); sort later. Friction kills
   capture.
2. **Atomic notes** — one idea per note, in your own words, with a clear
   title.
3. **Link, don't file** — connect related notes; folders are for archives,
   links are for thinking.
4. **Source everything** — every fact keeps its source URL or reference.
5. **Review weekly** — 15 minutes: process the inbox, surface open loops,
   delete the stale.
6. **Write to think** — a weekly 200-word summary of what you learned beats
   a thousand clipped highlights.
